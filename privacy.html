Privacy

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Mermaid Editor is a free tool with no accounts or sign-ups. We never ask for your name, email address or any other personal detail.

Your diagrams stay in your browser. Rendering and exports to SVG or PNG happen entirely on your device. Nothing you type is uploaded or stored by us.

Who receives data. The site is hosted by Vercel, which processes requests (including IP addresses) to deliver the page. The Mermaid library is loaded from jsDelivr and fonts from Google Fonts; both receive your IP address when your browser fetches those files. There are no analytics, ads, cookies or tracking, and nothing is sold.
